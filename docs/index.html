Hold the Mine

Isometric tower defense + management · Unity 6 · WebGL

You are the soldier in charge of a gold mine under siege. Fight in real time, place barricades, soldiers and towers, keep the miners fed and morale high, and survive 10 raider waves coming from the open desert, the dense forest and the road.

Play in your browser
Controls
WASD / Mouse / Left clickMove / aim / shoot
RReload
1 / 2 / 3Build barricade / soldier / tower (left click place, right click cancel, scroll rotates)
4 / 5Order food / hire a miner
Left click a unitSelect it (info panel, upgrade)
Right click groundSend the selected soldier there to hold position
X / U / MRelease soldier / upgrade selection / select the mine
Q / FRally nearby troops / send nearby soldiers to the cursor
Tab / EscShop / deselect or pause

One-Way Ticket

Third-person survival and escape · Three.js · plays in the browser

They answered a job advert and were sent to a war. Play as Kwame or Brian, two men recruited with fake job offers and held in a camp near the front. Survive the cold, hide what you find from the guards, dodge drones and shelling, and find a way through the fence and the minefield.

Play in your browser
Controls
WASD (ZQSD on AZERTY) / MouseMove / look around
ETake, talk, hide items, feed the stove, sleep · hold E to cut the fence
R / C / ShiftEat / crouch / run
Right click + left clickAim and shoot (rifle)
H / PHelp / pause
Desktop browser with keyboard and mouse recommended. Source: github.com/leogrzn/Leo-s-game-prototypes
